SNOW KINGDOM: SIDE QUEST OVERHAUL (publish to PhoenixOfTheSpoon/wilderland-arcade, bump BUILD)

Right now there are only 6 side story chains (heist, trials, lokiball, baumem, mowtale, bard), and every quest is built from 6 step types (go, items, chase, boss, waves, course). Make side quests a huge part of the Snow Kingdom, on the CURRENT map, before the world expansion.

═══ 1. NEW QUEST STEP TYPES ═══
Add these to the side quest engine (keep the 6 existing ones):
- escort: protect someone walking a route; they can be knocked down and need helping up.
- stealth: sneak past guards with view cones; getting spotted restarts the step, not the quest.
- defend: hold a spot for a timer against waves.
- puzzle: ice-block pushing, light beams, rune order.
- timed delivery: carry something fragile or warm across the map before it breaks or cools.
- investigate: collect clues into a clue board, then pick the right answer (wrong answers give hints, never fail the quest).
- choice: dialogue choices with real consequences (different reward, a character's fate, a different ending line).
- track: follow footprints, sounds or a scent trail.
- photo: frame something with a camera mode.
- fish / cook: mini-tasks using fishing and cooking.
- follow: tail someone without being seen or losing them.
- talk-around: ask several villagers.

- Spread them across all current regions, villages and the lands beyond.
- Each one uses at least 2 different step types; chains end with something big (a choice, a boss following the boss rules, a reveal).
- A mix of funny, heartwarming, spooky and sad. Dark is fine, no gore.
- Use the cast, and keep the lore exact:

═══ 3. QUEST SYSTEM UPGRADES ═══
- Journal (J): tabs for Main, Side, Daily and Completed; filters by region; quest steps with hints; "Track" button; how many side quests are done per region.
- Quest markers:
  - purple "!" for new quests, "?" for turn-ins;
  - the tracked quest shows a direction arrow and a map marker;
  - hidden quests have no marker until you find their note or clue.
- Hidden quests: at least 8, started by finding a note, an odd object or overhearing villagers.
- Wandering quests: travelers who appear at random spots for a while with a short quest.
- Village quest boards with 3 daily side jobs each, resetting daily (never punishing if missed).
- Reputation per village: helping raises it and unlocks shop items, cosmetics and a special quest at max.
- NPC schedules: some quest givers move around (home at night, market in the day).
- Choices are remembered: characters mention what you did later.
- Companion banter: teammates and Mau Mau comment during quests.
- Rewards:
  - Frost Crystals, gear, mount skins, base decorations, titles, outfits, lore pages, Wren songs;
  - chain finales give a unique reward;
  - rewards scale with Threat where it makes sense.
- Side quest bosses follow the boss rules (telegraphed attacks, safe gaps, breathers, projectile cap, real mechanics) and use Threat and the damage stacking system.
